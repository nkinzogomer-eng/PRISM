## Important scope note

The exact solver assumes a uniform prior over the currently compatible hypotheses and deterministic observations. This is an explicit reference assumption, not a universal PRISM rule. A future probabilistic benchmark must provide its prior and observation model directly.

The exact solver is an oracle for small instances. It is not the scalable PRISM selector and its planning cost must be reported separately from the optimal execution cost.

## AI boundary

PRISM's certified path is independent of AI. An AI may propose operations or summaries, but it cannot define truth, alter evidence, accept certificates, or silently expand assumptions. Every AI-assisted run needs a deterministic fallback or abstention.
