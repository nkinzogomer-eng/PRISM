# PRISM Journal: 2026-10-10

## Decision

The project will pursue PRISM as an adaptable algorithmic core, not as a replacement for fuzzers, symbolic executors, astronomy pipelines, or other domain tools.

## Objective

PRISM should discover hidden, decision-relevant distinctions in a problem and use the cheapest justified operations to separate competing explanations. It must return either a certified decision or an explicit abstention.

## Why this scope

The original SCM intuition was valuable but too broad. Many proposed components already exist independently. A credible contribution must therefore be tested at the level of a precise controller and a new capability, not at the level of branding a collection of known optimizations.

## Non-negotiable constraints

- No claim of universal superiority.
- No assumption that a smaller hypothesis set means the right answer.
- No hidden oracle access.
- No omission of planning, representation, preprocessing, or verification cost.
- No silent conversion of uncertainty into a confident answer.
- No large architecture before the kernel passes controlled tests.

## Initial kernel

The first kernel contains:

1. a problem contract;
2. a compatible-hypothesis state;
3. safe bounds and dependency summaries;
4. candidate operation generation;
5. cost-aware operation selection;
6. decision-invariance stopping;
7. independent witness verification;
8. structured abstention;
9. complete provenance and cost accounting.

## Research hypothesis

A domain-independent decision-distinction controller may discover useful structure that is missed by methods optimizing only hypothesis reduction, code coverage, or local information gain. This is a hypothesis, not an established fact.

## Immediate next test

Build a finite synthetic benchmark in which:

- multiple models agree on observed data;
- some models disagree on a target decision;
- observations have heterogeneous costs;
- exact optimal policies are computable on small instances;
- the true model can be intentionally omitted from the candidate class;
- parity and dense-dependency controls are included.

Compare PRISM against random selection, fixed order, information gain, decision gain, value of information, and exact optimal policy where tractable.

## Failure condition

If PRISM cannot produce a verified end-to-end advantage after all costs are included, the general controller claim must be reduced or abandoned. A benchmark-specific win is not enough.

## Next entry

Define the formal benchmark schema and implement the smallest reference simulator before adding domain adapters.
